# Playbook: explainer promo
> Explain a concept (for example OpenTelemetry signals) and land on the product as the answer.

1. Concept in 3 beats, each with one diagram scene (shapes, mono labels), style `pastel-explainer` or `prompt-motion`.
2. Product scene with a real screenshot showing the concept in practice.
3. End card with CTA and question. Landscape for YouTube, vertical cut for Shorts.
