# Playbook: product Short (real dashboard footage)
> A 30 to 45 s vertical Short built on real screenshots or a real screen recording of the product.

1. Pain-first hook line (for example "Your AI agent said Done. It lied.").
2. Reproduce the situation for real (run the app, generate real traces) and capture stills at 3x or a scroll recording.
3. Template `short-vertical`, style `cinematic` or `pastel-explainer`. 5 to 7 scenes, each 4 to 7 s.
4. Captions burned in, voice at 1.25x, -14 LUFS.
5. Post to YouTube Shorts, then LinkedIn with the YouTube card, then Slack with links in the thread.
