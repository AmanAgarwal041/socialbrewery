# Review checklist (run before every render and every post)

- [ ] Hook lands in 3 s, product name not before the hook.
- [ ] Every screenshot is real and current (from main, not an old branch). Illustrative shots are labeled.
- [ ] Button text is readable (OpenLIT: white on #C2410C).
- [ ] No em or en dashes in captions, titles or posts.
- [ ] Text inside safe areas on both cuts. No overlaps, no clipped words.
- [ ] Voice matches captions word for word. Audio at -14 LUFS, no clipping.
- [ ] End card: logo or loader, CTA link, open question.
- [ ] Vertical and landscape cuts both exported, plus `.srt` and poster.
- [ ] Keyframe sheet reviewed at phone size.
