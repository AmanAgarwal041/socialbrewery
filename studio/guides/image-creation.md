# Image creation

- Product shots: real screenshots only (Playwright or browser capture, `deviceScaleFactor` 2 or 3, light or dark theme
  matching the video). Crop to the region you will talk about; keep a full frame too for the zoom-out.
- Thumbnails / posters: export a frame from the video at 2 to 3 s (after the hook lands) with the headline readable at
  phone size. 1280x720 for landscape, 1080x1920 for Shorts. Max 4 words on a thumbnail.
- Generated images (Grok Imagine or similar) are for abstract backgrounds or metaphors only, never fake UI, never real people.
  Label them as illustrative if they could be mistaken for the product.
- Keyframe sheets: before rendering, snapshot 4 to 8 key moments and review them (contrast, safe areas, typos).
- Logos: use the brand's SVG from the brand kit `logoUrl`. Never redraw a logo.
