# Prompt-motion inspired effects
> Snappy social-reel effects in the style of public prompt-motion.com reels: 2D-to-3D moments, light lines, slat reveals, kinetic type.

Our own descriptions, written from watching the reels (no content copied).

| Effect | Style source | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| 2D to 3D moment | madhav-xo | A flat UI card tilts into 3D space (rotationX 0 -> 50, z -> -400) to reveal depth, then snaps back flat. |
| Light line | madhav-xo | A 4 to 6 px accent line wipes across, everything collapses into it, the line carries into the next scene. |
| Kinetic headline | kyonax-on-tech | Words drop in one by one (y 80 -> 0, 0.25 s each, `expo.out`), key word swaps color to the accent. |
| Tech HUD labels | kyonax-on-tech | Mono labels with thin brackets point at UI areas, typed on character by character. |
| Slat reveal | misbahsy | The next screen appears through 6 to 10 vertical slats that rotate or scale in with a 0.04 s stagger. |
| Zoom-through | misbahsy | Push through a word or a logo into the next screen. |
| Counter roll | general | Real numbers roll up digit by digit (only real numbers). |

Use at most 3 different effects per 30 s so the video keeps one voice.
