# Product launch motion
> Clean SaaS launch: screenshots on a soft stage, feature headlines, cursor clicks, loader end card with a CTA.

- Screenshots sit on a rounded card (radius 24 px, soft shadow) on the brand background, slight 3D tilt (rotationY 6) that settles flat.
- Each feature = headline (top) + real screenshot + one focus pull + one cursor click with a ripple on the button.
- Cursor: a simple SVG arrow moving on `power2.inOut` paths, click = scale 0.9 plus accent ring.
- End: loader animation (template `scene-endcard.html`), logo, CTA URL (for example a cal.com booking link), and an open question.
- Length: landscape 45 to 70 s, vertical 30 to 60 s.
