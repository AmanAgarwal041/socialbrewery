# Pastel explainer
> Friendly vertical explainer: light paper background, pastel cards, thick outlines, mono labels, bouncy entries.

- Background paper `#FFF8F0`, ink `#1E1F33`, cards in 3 pastels (peach, mint, lilac) with a 4 px ink outline and a hard 8 px offset shadow.
- Entries `back.out(1.7)`, 0.35 s, stagger 0.08. Exits are quick fades with y -20.
- Big emoji-free icons drawn as simple shapes. Mono labels in caps.
- Works for "3 things", "before / after" and "how it works" Shorts.
