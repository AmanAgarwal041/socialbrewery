# Soft pop (clay-style)
> Friendly, tactile motion: blocks squash in and settle like soft clay, on warm flat colors.

Good for consumer products, community announcements, tips, and playful explainers.

- Effect: `pop` on most scenes. Every block (kicker, headline, number, screenshot, button) squashes in with a small elastic settle, staggered top to bottom.
- Colors: a warm, light brand kit reads best (for example `bg` #FFF4E6, `ink` #2B2118, `accent` #FF7A59, `card` #FFFFFF). Pass it as the brand, or save it with `save_brand_kit`.
- Keep shapes round: screenshots already sit on rounded cards; prefer short words and one highlight per line.
- Mix in `counter` for one real number and `wipe` for a section change; finish with `endcard`.
- Timing: 2.5 to 4 s per scene. Pop is busy, so give text at least 2 s on screen after it settles.
- This is code-drawn motion graphics, not clay footage or 3D characters. For realistic footage, use a video model clip instead.
